# Crypto Fundamental Analysis

This folder contains a structured framework for evaluating cryptocurrency projects based on their fundamentals rather than short-term price movements.

## Analysis Areas

### 1. Project

* Problem being solved
* Solution
* Use cases
* Competitive advantage

### 2. Technology

* Blockchain architecture
* Scalability
* Security
* Decentralization
* Innovation

### 3. Tokenomics

* Total supply
* Circulating supply
* Distribution
* Inflation
* Unlock schedule
* Token utility

### 4. Adoption

* Users
* Developers
* Partnerships
* Ecosystem growth
* Real-world usage

### 5. Team & Development

* Team background
* Development activity
* Roadmap
* Community

### 6. Market

* Market size
* Competition
* Market position
* Growth potential

### 7. Risks

* Regulatory risk
* Technology risk
* Competition
* Token supply risk
* Adoption risk
* Security risk

## Objective

The goal is to build a systematic framework for understanding the long-term strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and risks of crypto projects.

> This repository is for research and educational purposes only and is not financial advice.
